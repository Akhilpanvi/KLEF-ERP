L = Lecture, P = Practical, S = Skill. "Weighted %" is the ERP's own figure. "Rest of semester" uses your weekly timetable (reconstructed below) and the academic calendar: it counts the classes each slot will still hold until the last instruction day, skipping Sundays, holidays and exam weeks, and discounts the usual cancellation rate.
